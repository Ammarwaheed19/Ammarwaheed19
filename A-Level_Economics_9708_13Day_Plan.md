## 🧮 FORMULAS & DEFINITIONS SHEET

**Elasticity:** PED = %ΔQd/%ΔP · XED = %ΔQd(A)/%ΔP(B) · YED = %ΔQd/%ΔY · PES = %ΔQs/%ΔP
**Multiplier:** k = 1/(1-MPC) = 1/MPW
**MPC + MPS + MPT + MPM = 1**
**GDP:** Y = C + I + G + (X-M)
**Fisher:** MV = PT
**Real GDP:** Nominal GDP × (100 / Price Index)
**Real Interest Rate:** Nominal - Inflation
**Terms of Trade:** (Index of export prices / Index of import prices) × 100
**Marshall-Lerner:** PEDx + PEDm > 1 (for devaluation to improve BoP)
**HDI components:** Life expectancy, Education (mean + expected years), GNI per capita
**Gini coefficient:** 0 = perfect equality, 1 = perfect inequality

## 🎯 ESSAY STRUCTURE (25-marker) — memorize this

1. **Define** all key terms (2 min)
2. **Diagram** — always draw one if relevant (label everything)
3. **KAA** — Knowledge, Application, Analysis — 3 well-developed points
4. **Evaluation** — for each point, offer a counter (short-run vs long-run, ceteris paribus, depends on elasticity, depends on time lag, depends on the economy)
5. **Conclusion** — take a side, justify it briefly, mention "it depends on…"

**Golden evaluation phrases:**
- "However, this depends on the elasticity of demand…"
- "In the short run… but in the long run…"
- "This assumes ceteris paribus, which may not hold because…"
- "Magnitude of impact depends on…"
- "There is a risk of government failure…"
- "Time lags may reduce effectiveness…"
