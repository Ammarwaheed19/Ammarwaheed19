/ About

A working factory, not a trading desk.

Pearl International has manufactured leather safety gloves and protective workwear in Karachi since 1992, supplying distributors, importers and private-label brands worldwide.

Our story

Three decades of one trade.

Founded in 1992 in Karachi, Pakistan, Pearl International is a manufacturer and bulk supplier of industrial safety products and protective workwear. We have built our business on consistent quality, reliable supply and long-term B2B partnerships.

Our factory handles large-scale production while keeping inspection in-house. From leather welding gloves to industrial coveralls, every article is made to protect workers in demanding environments, and every article is checked by our own team before it leaves the floor.
