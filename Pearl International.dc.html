Our story

Manufacturing excellence since 1992.

Pearl International is a leading manufacturer and bulk supplier of industrial safety products and protective workwear. Founded in 1992 in Karachi, Pakistan, we have built a reputation for consistent quality, reliable supply and strong B2B partnerships across the globe.

Our facilities handle large-scale production while maintaining strict quality control. We serve distributors, importers and industrial companies across the UK, Australia, Europe, the UAE and beyond.

From premium leather welding gloves to industrial coveralls, every product reflects our commitment to quality, durability and worker safety.

Manufacturing

Manufacturing facilities need comprehensive protective apparel across production lines, assembly and quality control. Our products are designed for extended wear in multi-hazard environments.

Key requirements
  • Machinery protection
  • Chemical resistance
  • Durability for reuse
  • Professional appearance

Construction

Construction sites present multiple hazards at once. Our heavy-duty apparel helps companies maintain safety standards while providing durable comfort for demanding job sites globally.

Key requirements
  • Heavy-duty durability
  • Debris protection
  • Weather resistance
  • Hi-vis compliance

Oil & Gas

The oil and gas sector demands the highest safety standards. Our protective apparel meets the stringent requirements of upstream, midstream and downstream operations.

Key requirements
